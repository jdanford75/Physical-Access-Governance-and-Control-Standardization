# Training & Control Assurance Plan

**Fictional, employer-neutral demonstration**

## Training model

1. Explain the policy's purpose, authorization roles and decision boundaries.
2. Practice hypothetical missing-approval, record-discrepancy and exception situations.
3. Assess whether the trainee identifies the authorized decision maker, necessary evidence and appropriate documentation.
4. Record acknowledgment of the approved version.
5. Use constructive coaching: observed gap → expected standard → practice → follow-up review.
6. Use quality checks to determine whether problems persist across shifts.

## Proposed measures (not actual results)

| Metric | Proposed measurement |
| --- | --- |
| Training currency | Current acknowledgments / assigned staff |
| Record completeness | Sampled decisions with all required fields |
| Exception review | Exceptions reviewed before expiration |
| Approval traceability | Sampled decisions linked to authorizing role |
| Repeat errors | Similar documentation omissions after coaching |

## Fictional coaching example

**Learning need:** A trainee recorded the outcome but not the authorization rationale.

**Action:** Explain the documentation expectation and have the trainee complete a simulated case.

**Follow-up:** Review the sample entry for clarity, accuracy and minimal necessary personal information.

No actual employee records, site-specific procedures or confidential details are included.
