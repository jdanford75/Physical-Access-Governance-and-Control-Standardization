# Illustrative Access Decision & Evidence Matrix

| Hypothetical scenario | Governance concern | Authorized reviewer | Evidence to retain under policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Access request lacks approval | Unauthorized access risk | Assigned approver | Request ID, approval or denial and timestamp |
| Identity information conflicts | Verification uncertainty | Trained supervisor or designated authority | Limited discrepancy note, verification outcome, decision |
| Staff member requests an urgent waiver | Uncontrolled exception | Authorized exception owner | Justification, approver, expiry and follow-up |
| Person changes job duties | Excessive privilege | Access owner | Access review, amendment/removal ticket |
| Different staff reach inconsistent decisions | Unclear procedures or training | Control owner | Anonymized case sample, coaching and retest record |
| Expired access remains active | Delayed deprovisioning | Access administrator and owner | Expiry, revocation evidence and review result |

## Example finding (fictional)

**Observation:** A sample of access records lacks documented approval references.

**Risk:** Decisions cannot be consistently traced to delegated authority.

**Proposed correction:** Improve workflow fields, train staff, and sample records again after implementation.

**Status:** Example only; no actual audit fieldwork or remediation was performed.
