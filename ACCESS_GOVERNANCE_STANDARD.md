# Illustrative Physical Access Governance Standard

**Status:** Sample draft for a fictional organization; not an approved workplace policy.

## Objective

Establish fair, consistent, documented authorization decisions rather than relying on personal familiarity, urgency, or individual discretion. Make security decisions reviewable by a designated control owner.

## Governance roles

- **Sponsor:** States the legitimate business need for access.
- **Approver:** Authorizes or rejects access within delegated authority.
- **Security personnel:** Validate identity and authorization using the organization's *approved* process and escalate unresolved issues.
- **Access administrator:** Implements approved permissions and removes expired privileges.
- **Control owner:** Maintains guidance, reviews exceptions and audits decision consistency.
- **Privacy/legal/HR:** Review proportional personal-data handling and applicable workforce requirements.

## General control principles

1. Confirm a legitimate business purpose and authorization from the appropriate authority.
2. Separate identification from authorization. Possessing an identifier alone does not grant permission.
3. Resolve unclear records through an authorized review process, not an unofficial workaround.
4. Document material exceptions with business justification, approver, duration, owner and review date.
5. Record only necessary personal information and protect it under approved retention requirements.
6. Review access when a person's role changes, access expires, or participation ends.
7. Keep revised standards version-controlled and acknowledge changes through training.
8. Review process deviations to distinguish a training problem from an unclear or ineffective control.

## Approval and continuous review

A draft becomes policy only after appropriate organizational review and approval. Review control effectiveness using selected records, interviews, exception trends and documented corrective-action follow-up.

*Illustrative process only. Does not reveal real-site access instructions or bypass procedures.*
